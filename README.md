# Vows In Motion Studio

Design a premium, modern marketing website for a digital wedding invitation business. This is the storefront design only — not an individual invitation page.



THE BUSINESS

We sell digital wedding invitation microsites to Indian couples. A couple picks one of 16+ designs, customises it free (names, dates, venue, functions, photos), and pays a one-time ₹599 fee to publish and share a link on WhatsApp. Each design, once opened by a guest, is an animated phone experience: an envelope or doors or curtains open, music fades in, a scratch-off card reveals the date, there's a live countdown. Think invitima.com and zareqia.com as competitors, but we want to look more premium and modern than both.



WHAT TO DESIGN

1. Homepage: hero section, a showcase of the invitation designs (shown inside realistic phone mockups), a "how it works" section (3 steps: choose a design, customise free, pay to publish), a pricing section (₹599 one-time, no subscription), footer.

2. A template gallery page showing many designs as cards (each card needs to display a phone mockup preview, a title, a short tagline, and a "Use this design" button).

3. A simple top navigation (logo/wordmark placeholder "Aarzoo", links: Designs, How it works, Pricing, a "Create yours" button).



STYLE DIRECTION

- Premium and modern — NOT a generic SaaS template, NOT a typical AI-startup gradient-and-rounded-card look.

- Warm, editorial, wedding-appropriate: think luxury bridal magazine meets modern product design. Confident serif typography paired with a clean sans. Generous whitespace. Tasteful use of gold or warm accent colour, used sparingly as a signature rather than everywhere.

- Real depth and texture — subtle shadows, refined borders, maybe a soft paper or linen grain somewhere, nothing flat or cookie-cutter.

- The phone mockups showing invitation previews should look like genuinely premium device renders (like real Apple product photography) — not a plain grey rounded rectangle.

- Mobile-first responsive, must look excellent on a phone since most visitors will come from Instagram on mobile.



Use placeholder content/images for the invitation designs shown in phone mockups (romantic wedding imagery, florals, arches — tasteful stock-style placeholders is fine for now, we will swap in real designs later).



Build this as a working React + Tailwind site. Make it feel like a real premium brand, not a demo.
